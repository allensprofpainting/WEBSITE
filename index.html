What I do

Painting, done properly.

The finish is only as good as what's underneath it. Every job gets the same prep, the same quality paint and the same care — whatever the size.

01

Interior Painting

Walls, ceilings, trims and doors. Furniture and floors protected, surfaces properly prepped, clean sharp lines.

02

Exterior Painting

Weatherboards, render, fascia, eaves and trims. Bayside salt air and Queensland sun are hard on paint — the prep is what makes it last.

03

Patch & Paint Repairs

Water-damaged ceilings, cracks, holes and cornice repairs — fixed and painted so you can't pick where the damage was.

04

Feature Walls & Texture

Metallics, texture coatings and Dulux AcraTex finishes. A specialist skill most painters don't offer.

05

Decks, Rails & Fences

Timber stripped back, prepared and finished to stand up to the weather — not just a coat over the top.

06

Doors, Trims & Spray Finishes

Sprayed doors, gates and metalwork for a smooth, even finish that a brush and roller can't match.
